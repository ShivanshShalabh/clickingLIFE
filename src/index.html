Why I keep clicking

Most of these were taken on regular days: a walk to class, a cold bus stop, ice on a railing.

Ordinary now. Worth a lot later.

So I take the picture anyway.

If you have four minutes, listen to Ed Sheeran's Photograph. It's about how a picture holds on to a moment after the moment is gone, and watch the video while it plays: it's all home videos of him as a kid, clips nobody thought were special when they were filmed. That's exactly why they're special now.

Every photo here was taken on an ordinary day. Embed a random one anywhere with /api/random, or list them with /api/photos.
